Our Services

Three focused programs, one continuous relationship.

Every patient starts with a consultation and diagnosis, then moves into one or more of the programs below — built around where you are, not a fixed package.

Hepatitis B & C Treatment Detox Programs Liver Health Support
Core Program

Hepatitis B & C Treatment

Natural-based therapies to support and manage liver health for patients affected by chronic viral hepatitis, paired with ongoing monitoring.

This is where most patients start. After your consultation and diagnosis, we build a protocol around your specific viral load, liver function, and general health — combining herbal medicine, nutritional support, and lifestyle adjustments aimed at strengthening your immune response and protecting your liver.

Treatment length varies by patient. We track progress at each follow-up and adjust the plan as your results and symptoms change, rather than running everyone through a fixed timeline.

What's Included

  • Initial consultation and diagnostic review
  • Personalized herbal and nutritional protocol
  • Scheduled follow-ups to track liver function
  • Direct access to your care team between visits

Who it's for: Patients newly diagnosed with Hepatitis B or C, or currently managing either condition and looking for a structured, natural-based plan.

Add-on Program

Detox Programs

Guided detoxification protocols aimed at cleansing the body, easing the load on your liver, and rebuilding energy levels.

Detox is offered as a standalone program or alongside hepatitis treatment, depending on what your consultation turns up. It's built around your current diet, energy levels, and any symptoms you're carrying — not a one-size regimen.

You'll get clear guidance on what to eat, drink, and avoid during the program, plus check-ins to track how your body's responding.

What's Included

  • Detox protocol tailored to your current health status
  • Nutrition and hydration guidance
  • Energy and symptom check-ins through the program
  • Can run alongside hepatitis treatment or on its own

Who it's for: Patients wanting to support liver function and energy levels, whether or not they're currently in hepatitis treatment.

Ongoing Care

Liver Health Support

Longer-term therapies and monitoring for patients managing liver-related conditions after their initial treatment ends.

Treatment doesn't stop at your last dose. This program is for staying ahead of your liver health over time — regular check-ins, dietary and lifestyle adjustments, and early flagging of anything that needs closer attention.

Most patients move into this program after completing hepatitis treatment, though it's also available on its own for anyone managing a liver-related condition long-term.

What's Included

  • Regular liver function check-ins
  • Lifestyle and dietary adjustments for long-term liver health
  • Early flagging of changes that need attention
  • Available standalone or after completing treatment

Who it's for: Patients who've completed treatment and want ongoing monitoring, or anyone managing a liver condition long-term.

Before You Book

Every program is built around your own diagnosis and how you respond over time. We'll walk you through what to realistically expect for your specific case during your consultation, rather than promising a fixed outcome up front.

Not sure which program fits?

Book a consultation and we'll recommend a starting point based on your diagnosis and history.
