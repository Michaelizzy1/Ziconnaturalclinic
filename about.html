About Zicon Clinic

A clinic built around one condition, and the patients living with it.

For over a decade, Zicon Clinic has focused on Hepatitis B and C care in Abakaliki — pairing natural, evidence-informed therapy with the kind of continuity that comes from treating one thing closely, not everything at once.

"

Most patients come to us after being told to manage a diagnosis for life. Our job is to sit with that, take it seriously, and build a plan around the person — not the paperwork.

Zicon Clinic started from a simple observation: people living with Hepatitis B and C needed somewhere that treated the condition as an ongoing relationship, not a single appointment. That's shaped everything since — from how we run consultations to how long we stay in touch after treatment begins.

We combine natural, holistic therapies with attentive medical monitoring, and we're upfront about what a treatment plan can and can't promise. Every patient's course is different, so every plan is built from their diagnosis, their liver function, and how they respond over time — not a fixed protocol applied to everyone who walks in.

That's the standard we hold ourselves to, and it's why patients tend to stay with us well past their first visit.

Dr. Shadrach Ngwuta
Founder & CEO, Zicon Clinic
What Guides Our Care

Four principles behind every treatment plan.

01

Root-cause focus

We look past symptoms to what's driving them — liver function, immune response, and lifestyle factors — rather than treating each flare-up in isolation.

02

Personalized plans

No two patients receive an identical protocol. Your plan is built around your diagnosis, your progress, and how your body responds along the way.

03

Plain-language transparency

You'll always know what's in your treatment plan and why — explained in terms you can act on, not medical shorthand.

04

Confidentiality

Your diagnosis and records stay private, discussed only between you and your care team, at a pace you're comfortable with.
